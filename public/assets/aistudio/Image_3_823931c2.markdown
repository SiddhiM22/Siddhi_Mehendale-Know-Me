## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-caliber engineering leadership, architectural clarity, and forward-looking craft. Targeted directly at technical recruiters, engineering VPs, and founders, the interface avoids generic developer portfolio tropes in favor of an elite, executive-level technical narrative.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalist structure with deep-space glassmorphism and surgical technical detailing:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, luminescent slate foundations that feel dimensional rather than pitch-black.
- **Precision:** Monospaced data points, structural hairline dividers, and quantified impact metrics balanced against warm, expansive sans-serif typography.
- **Restraint:** High-contrast accent colors deployed strictly for focal anchors, interaction feedback, and vital telemetry.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high legibility on pitch and slate backgrounds, maintaining strict WCAG AAA contrast ratios for reading text while reserving luminous spectral tones for focal anchors.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** Primary calls-to-action, key milestone highlights, and primary active states.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6` - Emerald Teal):** Production status badges, verified credentials, and high-impact metric counters.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Cyan Ray):** Interactive code elements, data visualization accents, and tertiary metadata tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Root background tone, layered downward into `#090D16` for canvas recesses and elevated into `#1E293B` for surface containers.

### Layered Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#090D16`
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Container):** `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.70)` with backdrop blur.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hover/Active Panes):** `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.55)`
- **Border Stroke Subtle:** `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`
- **Border Stroke Focus:** `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.40)`
- **Text High-Emphasis:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Text Medium-Emphasis:** `#94A3B8`
- **Text Low-Emphasis / Code Commentary:** `#64748B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy distinguishes structural roles through three deliberate type families:

1. **Space Grotesk:** Headlines and numerical impact stats. Its geometric incisions convey technological confidence without looking abrasive.
2. **Geist:** Primary UI, long-form project case studies, and executive summaries. Clean, neutral, and engineered for dense legibility on high-resolution screens.
3. **JetBrains Mono:** Tech stacks, metadata, commit logs, performance benchmarks, and micro-labels.

### Micro-Typographic Rules
- `label-caps` must always be styled with `text-transform: uppercase` to maintain crisp scan-lines in dense recruiter-facing resumes.
- Quantitative career achievements (e.g., "$4.2M ARR", "-42% Latency") combine Space Grotesk bold numbers with JetBrains Mono unit symbols.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around maximum content readability and rapid scanning.
